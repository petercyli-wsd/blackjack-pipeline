Privacy, session limits & uploads

Keys stay in this server's memory, never on disk and never shared with other users. They expire after 8 hours; use Clear keys or End my session to remove them sooner. When the app runs on your computer, keys you enter here are also used by your pipeline runs in the background.

Videos go to this app's processing server. Generation sends boundary images to fal/KIE; optional AI review also sends reference images and sampled frames across the bridge and both joins through fal/OpenRouter. Download results before ending your session. Single-pair limits: 512 MB per video, 1 GB uploads and 5 renders per session; clips up to 10 minutes. CSV batches recycle a separate workspace (two sources and, for existing-output review, one result) after each saved row. Batch checkpoints and buffered ZIPs stay in this browser until you clear them.

Quality review

Uses your fal key for Claude Opus 5.5 visual review of reference images and timestamped frames across the bridge and both joins. Two independent checks must agree before an automatic redo. Uncertain results need a person; AI approval is not a guarantee.

Prototype tolerance: a clean pass needs at least 70% confidence. A major defect still blocks approval; automatic redos require 85% confidence from both checks. Minor imperfections alone are acceptable. Updated thresholds apply to new reviews, including pending rows when you resume. Earlier saved flags keep their original verdict.

Redo limit & appearance policy

Default: 1 redo. With 2 redos, the first replacement is reviewed; a second is generated only if the first still has a confirmed major defect. Stop on a pass or uncertainty. Existing-folder review starts with your current file, so 2 redos means at most 2 new videos per row. Reviews and generations use credits.

Reskin mode still rejects warped hands, body/sleeve/hair geometry, occlusion errors and changing cards. Automatic redos use the same Kling/Seedance method and duration. FILM, RIFE and local methods are flagged for manual review instead of repeating the same interpolation.

Reviews and redos use extra credits. Uncertain clips need human review. In-place repair keeps their originals; copy modes save them under needs-review/.

Audio & output details

Source audio is kept with short fades around silent bridges. Dissolves crossfade audio. Output fits clip A, up to 1920 px wide.
