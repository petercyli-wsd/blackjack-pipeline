Privacy, session limits & uploads

Keys stay in server memory for your browser session, never saved or shared with other users. Keys expire after 8 hours; idle sessions and files expire after 2 hours. Use Clear keys or End my session to remove them.

Videos go to this app's processing server; only boundary images go to fal/KIE. Download results before ending your session. Single-pair limits: 512 MB per video, 1 GB uploads and 5 renders per session; clips up to 10 minutes. CSV batches recycle a separate two-video workspace after each saved row.
